04 · Privacy

Your setup.
Your choices.

Where your data lives

TRQYOS is designed as a desktop assistant. Where files, logs and other data are stored depends on your configuration; check your build and settings before using sensitive information.

No account required

Installation and setup depend on the build you use.

Local options may be available

Some actions designed to work locally, while API providers or connected services requires an internet connection.

Know what uses a connection

If you use an online model or external service, review its settings and privacy terms to understand what data may be sent.

05 · FAQ

Questions.

A few things to know before getting started.

Does TRQYOS work offline?

Yes, If you are using local LLMs.

Can it damage my files?

No, if you didn't allow it.

Which apps can it control?

App control depends on the tools and integrations available in your build.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 11 and all Linux distros.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free and open source.
